Added while (getchar() != '\n'); immediately after scanf("%c", &choice); to take the leftover newline character left in the input buffer by scanf("%c"). Without this, the fgets call for the plaintext message would immediately read the newline as an empty line, skipping the string input entirely.
Added buffer[strcspn(buffer, "\n")] = '\0'; after fgets(buffer, sizeof(buffer), file); in the decrypt branch. Without this, the trailing newline written by fprintf(file, "%s\n", plaintext) during encryption was being included in the decrypt XOR operation, corrupting the last character of recovered output.
